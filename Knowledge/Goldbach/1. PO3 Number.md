## What are Power of Three Numbers?

Power of three numbers are simply a base number three raised to an integer exponent. The exponent tells you how many times to multiply three by itself.

- Exponent zero returns number 1.
- Exponent one gives 3.
- Exponent two gives 9 (3 * 3).
- Exponent three gives 27 (3 * 3 * 3).
- And so on.

As of this video, ==**no asset requires an exponent higher than 11**== (3^11 = 177,147), which applies to Bitcoin at its widest ranges. This gives a compact set of just 11 key power of three levels. Most traders only actively use two to three of these levels for their specific asset.
