# Transcript

We outlined the market structure shift.

This happens when a short-term swing low or high is traded through. Ideally, this short-term swing should be close to a goldback level, and there must be a gap located at the goldback level.

The market shift occurs when price closes above or below the Goldbok level. You will see a retrace towards the Goldbok level and gap. This is where you enter.

You close the trade when it reaches the middle of the range in between the current Gold Bock levels, and the next one. You can do partials, but you should take something off.

A lot of times price rejects the middle of the range and returns back to the gold box level. It will run stops again, reject the level to continue its path towards the next gold box level.
