# Summary
## What PO3 Stop Runs Are

PO3 stop runs refer to price movements engineered by smart money to capture liquidity. They target specific extension levels derived from the Power of Three (PO3) number system. These levels often occur at swing highs and swing lows.

## Key PO3 Numbers

- Common PO3 numbers: 9, 27, 81, 243, 729 handles.
    
- The higher the time frame, the larger the PO3 number. For example, on a weekly chart, swings might run by 729 handles.
    
- In day trading, focus on 9 or 27 handles; 81 handle runs appear in high volatility conditions.
    

## Measurement and Application

- Use a measurement tool to identify the extension created to tag liquidity. The extension itself is a PO3 number (e.g., 27 or 81) before price reverses.
    
- Liquidity pools exist above swing highs and below swing lows. Goldbach levels can help tag that liquidity.
    

## PO3 Dealing Range Stop Run

- An external range liquidity run targets level 11.
    
- 11% of a PO3 dealing range is itself a PO3 number. For instance, 11% of 243 equals 27 (rounded), and 81 is the PO3 stop run of a 729 range.
