# Summary

## Optimal Dealing Range Concept

- The Power of Three (PO3) dealing range is used to assess order flow. Choosing too small a number increases whipsaw risk; too large may miss swing points.
    
- Higher time frames require larger PO3 numbers (e.g., 6561 or 18683 for quarterly/yearly analysis). Lower time frames need smaller numbers.
    
- Optimal range evolves gradually over weeks/months/years and remains stable once identified.
    

## Hallmark Signatures of Optimal Range

- Display a chart with ~10 trading days, overlay PO3 time frame (e.g., 27 min).
    
- Each distinct swing should intersect between 1 and 4 Goldbach levels.
    
    - If swings touch 0 or 1 level: PO3 too large → contract to a smaller PO3 number.
        
    - If swings span more than 4 levels: PO3 too small → expand to a larger PO3 number.
        

## Chart Examples

- NASDAQ: Using 729 PO3 dealing range and 27 PO3 time frame → swings span more than 4 Goldbach levels → too small.
    
- Using 6561 PO3 dealing range → swings touch only one level → too large. Solution: contract to 2187 PO3.
    
- With 2187 PO3, swings respect gold box levels. Zooming into lower PO3 time frame (9) shows gaps and blocks around levels → indicates optimal PO3 reached.
