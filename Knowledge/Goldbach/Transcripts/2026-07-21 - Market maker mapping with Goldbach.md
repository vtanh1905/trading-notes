# Summary
## Market Maker Models Overview

Market maker models, popularized by Inner Circle Trader (ICT) and described on the CME Group website, come in two forms: buy model and sell model. They typically start with consolidation around a golden ratio level, followed by a two-leg expansion, a fast move away, a retrace, and another fast expansion to a terminus at another golden ratio level where price reverses.

## Identifying Reversal and Entry

At the reversal point, look for confirmation (market structure shift or change of character). With golden ratio levels, this is the golden ratio inversion point at the 17 or 83 level, triggering a change in order flow direction. After identifying the starting consolidation and terminus, draw a fractal golden ratio Fibonacci between those two levels. The reversal pattern to look for is the Einstein trade plan using the 29 and 17 levels to automate the search for market structure shifts.

## Einstein Trade Plan Execution

When price hits the terminus golden ratio level, expect an aggressive move toward the flow layer. The 29 sweet spot is where it pauses to form a short-term swing. Price then retraces toward the terminus and builds the Einstein trade plan: look for a block and an expansion creating a gap between 11 to 17 levels (for longs) or 89 to 83 levels (for shorts). The expansion should reach the flow layer, preferably the sweet spot, before retracing into the gap. That gap is the entry zone with the LLOD protecting the trade. This pattern is often called the smart money reversal.

## Continuation and Exit

From there, the model continues with expansion through the rebalance layer toward the opposite flow layer, and ultimately to the range high (buy model) or range low (sell model). Use golden ratio levels from the new fractal fib to trail stops and ride the move back toward the original consolidation area.
