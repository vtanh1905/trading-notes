# Summary
## Introduction to the Green Trifecta Layer

Also known as the liquidity layer, this layer sits at the high and low of the PO3 dealing range. It features several key signatures.

## Key Goldbock Levels

Three Goldbock levels are active:

- Internal Rejection Level: 3 (discount) and 97 (premium)
- Internal Liquidity Level: 11 (discount) and 89 (premium)
- Goldbock Inversion Point (GIP): 17 (discount) and 83 (premium)

## Adjacent PO3 Dealing Ranges

Adjacent dealing ranges sit immediately below and above the current one. They are critical for understanding how stacked ranges work.

## Purpose of the Liquidity Layer

The liquidity layer is designed to engineer liquidity inside the current dealing range, only to later draw that liquidity outside it.

## Two Main Scenarios When Price Moves Beyond the Current Range

1. Price rejects the external range (above or below) and returns inside the original dealing range.
2. The current range is abandoned, and price shifts to the new range above or below.

## Basic Structure

When price approaches the high or low of a dealing range, it often falls just short of the extreme. This engineers liquidity. Two Goldbock levels are typically in play: 3 and 11 on the discount side, and 97 and 89 on the premium side. These create a swing low near the range low or a swing high near the range high, pushing price in the opposite direction. This generates internal range liquidity (IRL or IRD). Traders place stop losses just beyond the new swing, which price soon sweeps. Then price is driven to the range extreme and often outside toward the next dealing range, targeting 311 or 9789 levels. This is external range liquidity (ERL or ERD).

## Outcomes After External Liquidity

Either price rejects the external liquidity and reverses back into the original dealing range, or price fails to re-enter and begins trading within the new dealing range. In the latter case, zoom out to a larger PO3 range.

## Goldbock Inversion Point (GIP)

A third key level is 17/83, called the GIP. It will be explained in a dedicated video.

## Application

This dynamic plays out on PO3 dealing ranges and fractal ranges between two Goldbock levels, provided the PO3 size is large enough.
