# Summary

## Goldbock Levels Overview

Today we explore the fundamentals of goldbock levels. The goldbock trifecta relies on PO3 dealing ranges (broader dealing zones) and goldbock levels (critical institutional reference points). The focus is on turning these levels into a trading advantage.

## Key Reference Zones

Always watch for blocks, gaps, and liquidity around goldbock levels. These are high-probability reference zones.

## Concrete Example: Level 29

On the 27-minute PO3 timeframe, a gap (gray rectangle) is marked. Level 29 sits inside this gap and played a pivotal role throughout the trading day. The middle of the block lines up almost perfectly with the goldbock level, signaling level 29 as a major focal point.

## Price Action on 9-Minute Timeframe

The gap was first tested during Asian session, price rejected sharply, then drove lower through London and New York sessions. Support found at the liquidity just below the next goldbock level. A clean block formed around level 29. In the New York afternoon, price reversed off that goldblock level and rallied back into the block.

## Block Selection Rationale

The left block never touched the goldbach level; the right one did. The right block was prioritized.
