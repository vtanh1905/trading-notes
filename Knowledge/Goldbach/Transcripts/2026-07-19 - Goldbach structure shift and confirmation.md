# Summary

## Gold Box Level Patterns

In a downtrend, look for a gap above a gold box level. Price closes below the level (body close required), then retraces to fill the gap. This confirms bearish order flow and a structure shift. Later, a block should defend the level. Entry when price trades through the block; stop above the gap candle's high; target next gold box levels.

## Reversal Setup

In an uptrend, look for a gap below a gold box level. Price closes above the level, then retraces into the gap. This creates a clean order block and confirms a structure shift. Stop below the swing low that formed the gap; target higher gold box levels.

## Why It Works

The unfilled gap from the prior move creates anticipation of a return to fill it and reverse.

## Conclusion

Understanding PO3 dealing ranges, gold box levels, gaps, and blocks provides a high-probability trading system.
