# Summary

## Higher Time Frame Protocol

We rely on the power of three (PO3) across multiple timeframes. For longer-term direction, use proper PO3 daily time frames such as 9, 27, or 81 daily candles to get a clear view of the year ahead.

For price dealing ranges, use larger PO3 dealing ranges like 6,561 or 19,683 on the NQ to capture the full picture, especially with daily moves often exceeding 1,000 handles.

## Seasonal Tendencies

Analyze seasonal tendencies for your specific asset. For example, the NQ tends to consolidate early in the year, move lower from February through late March into early May, then expand away from a gold block level toward summer, frequently setting an intermediate high in August. Price then retraces through summer into September and October, expands again until late November, and consolidates into year-end.

Quarterly shifts: Winter drives prices lower, spring pushes higher, summer forms a high before retracing into autumn, and autumn drives prices up, setting the stage for winter consolidation. This creates the classic two-leg move in a bullish year.

Use an 81-day PO3 time frame chart with a dealing range of 6561 or 19683. Focus on the dealing range's highs, lows, and midpoint.

## Correlated Assets

Monitor correlated assets: for NQ, track S&P 500 and Dow Jones; for Bitcoin, watch Ethereum; for euro and other currencies, follow the inverse dollar; for gold, monitor the Australian dollar, etc.

When price reaches a significant level within a large enough PO3 range, look for clues of a seasonal high or low. Divergence among correlated assets provides clear signals: one or two assets may make a lower low while others do not, or one fails to make a higher high. Use the 81-day PO3 time frame to spot these divergences.

Example: December 2025 saw NQ fail to make a higher high while others did, suggesting a decline into spring. The following January 26th candle showed divergence with S&P 500 making a lower low while others did not, signaling a potential seasonal low. April 29th showed no divergence, supporting continued upside.

## Shifted Months Methodology

Apply the sequence of number nine to determine the shifted start day of a month. Key days: day 3 of the partition open (aligns with NFP), days 11, 17, 7, and 14. Day 20 brings consolidation. Use Goldbach partitions (triad of number three).

This approach lets you spot 3-4 major swings throughout the trading year, allowing account growth with just a handful of trades.
