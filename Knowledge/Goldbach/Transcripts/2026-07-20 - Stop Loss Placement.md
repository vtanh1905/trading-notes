# Summary
## Introduction

Stop loss placement is critical for long-term trading success. A common issue is knowing where to place the stop after entry. Without a stop, losses can be severe.

## The Goldbock System

A simple system using Goldbock levels to determine stop loss placement. When trading Goldbock levels, use the optimal PO3 dealing range for higher ranges and larger swings. Follow a risk one to reward two methodology: enter at a Goldbock level, risk one Goldbock level to target two Goldbock levels.

## Example: Long Trade

Enter long at the 17 level, with an Einstein plan premise. Stop loss is placed at the previous Goldbock level (11). Take profit targets are the next levels above: 23 and 29. Take partial profits along the way, and leave a runner to ultimately target the balance layer.

## Trade Management

Once the first Goldbock level is hit, move stop up to entry (17). This makes the trade risk-free. When next target is reached, take additional partials and trail stop one Goldbock level higher. Repeat. This method, with partial profits and trailing stops using previous Goldbock levels, leads to account growth.

## Advanced Tip

Ultra-aggressive traders can use mid levels between Goldbock levels for additional partials and stop moves. However, trailing stops too early reduces chances of a major winner.

## Conclusion

Goldbock levels provide a solid basis for stop loss placement. Review past trades to see how this system can improve placements.
