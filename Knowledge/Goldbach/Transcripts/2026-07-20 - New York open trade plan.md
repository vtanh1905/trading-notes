# Summary
## Trading Strategy Overview

This guide focuses on a high-probability pattern during the New York Open (9:30 AM to 12:00 PM) that reliably targets 25 handles. The strategy relies on small, consistent wins compounded with strict risk management.

## Technical Essentials

- Use the optimal PO3 dealing range.
    
- Plot goldbock levels (major institutional reference points).
    
- Analyze price action in the hours leading up to the New York open.
    

## Pre-Open Setup

Before 9:30 AM, identify stop runs that fall short of a gold box level. These failed sweeps usually produce swing highs or lows sized in PO3 increments (commonly 27 or 81 handles). Mark these obvious stop runs clearly on your chart.

## Entry Pattern

When New York opens, price often makes a classic stop run into a gold box level, clears stops, then reverses. Look for:

- Clean reversal 1 or reversal 2 patterns.
    
- Gold box level acting as strong support/resistance.
    
- If price is far from or slices through the gold box, the reversal two pattern becomes powerful. Check a 27PO3 stop run inside a 2187 dealing range.
    

Enter after confirmation. Stop loss just beyond the swing high/low formed during the liquidity sweep. Target 25 handles.

## Chart Examples

### Example 1

Mapped north and south liquidity pools. North side liquidity sat 27 handles into the goldbach level, south side slightly beyond 27. After open, price pushed north but failed, reversed to hunt south side. Swept into gold box, rejected, drove higher. Total 100 handles offered. Note: This was a counter-trend trade; keep position size conservative.

### Example 2

Before open, no short-term swings were 27 handles away from gold box. Using 81PO3 stop run, price went through gold-back level. But with 27 handles measured, reversal two likely. Price dipped below gold box, tagged south side liquidity, rejected, went back above. Long entry yielded 25 handles as over 100 handles offered.

### Example 3 (Advanced)

North and south liquidity pools both 27 handles short of gold box. After open, price ran toward south side but stalled and retraced, forming a textbook reversal two setup. Price pushed 27 handles above gold box, then reversed sharply, offering a clean short entry targeting south side liquidity below. Requires screen time to recognize.

## Key Takeaways

- Practice on charts daily.
    
- This setup becomes natural with consistent annotation and experience.
    
- Combine with strict risk management for steady account compounding.
